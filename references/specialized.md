# Specialized SEO

## Ecommerce

Inspect category and product intent separately. Check variant/canonical rules, faceted navigation, pagination, discontinued and out-of-stock behavior, product data accuracy, merchant feeds, reviews, category usefulness, and links from informational content to buying paths. Product/schema claims must match visible price, currency, availability, condition, identifiers, shipping, and return facts.

Avoid indexable combinations for every filter. Define which facets have distinct demand and unique value; keep the rest crawl-controlled or non-indexed according to platform constraints.

## Local SEO

Confirm whether the business is storefront, service-area, or hybrid and whether it has one or multiple locations. Compare visible name/address/phone, structured data, and supplied business profiles. Review location/service pages for genuine local value, not city-name swapping.

Cover business-profile completeness, categories, hours, reviews and responses, local citations, local links/press, maps/directions, click-to-call, and correct LocalBusiness subtype. Do not claim local-pack position without a location/device-aware measurement method. Follow platform policies; never recommend review gating or fake engagement.

## Programmatic SEO

Require evidence that templates satisfy distinct demand and can provide unique value at scale. Define data sources, template fields, uniqueness gates, indexation rules, internal-link generation, QA sampling, update ownership, and removal thresholds before producing pages.

Use staged rollout. Monitor crawl, indexation, duplication, engagement, conversions, and quality exceptions. Stop expansion when source data is incomplete, pages are substantially interchangeable, or the template creates doorway pages.

## Quality Gate

For any scalable page set, provide:

- unique user job and data advantage
- sample rendered pages before scale-up
- canonical/index policy for every state
- empty/duplicate/error handling
- editorial and technical QA sample plan
- monitoring and rollback criteria

# Authority, Links, and Digital PR

Use for backlink audits, link acquisition, reputation, citations, and off-page competitive analysis.

## Principles

- Evaluate relevance, editorial legitimacy, placement context, destination, and likely audience value before third-party authority scores.
- Brand mentions, expert citations, partnerships, original research, tools, and genuinely useful resources can support both discovery and trust.
- Avoid paid-link schemes, automated outreach spam, private network tactics, fake reviews, fabricated expert quotes, and guaranteed-ranking claims.

## Backlink Analysis

Use supplied or connected datasets; public search alone is not a complete backlink index. Segment links by destination, topic, link type, anchor pattern, acquisition period, and risk signals. Identify pages that already attract links and internal-link equity that is not reaching priority pages.

## Opportunity Qualification

For each prospect record relevance, audience overlap, editorial fit, suggested asset/pitch, target page, contact evidence, effort, and risk. Prefer a small qualified list over a large unverified directory dump.

## Digital PR Assets

Recommend assets with a defensible reason to cite: original datasets, transparent studies, calculators, benchmarks, expert commentary, timely analysis, or high-utility reference pages. State methodology and limitations.

## Reporting

Do not label a link toxic solely because a vendor score is high. Recommend disavowal only with strong evidence of manipulative links and after reviewing current search-engine guidance; routine low-quality links often require no action.
